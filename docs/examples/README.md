## How often do real opportunities come?
Three test careers (about six simulated years in all) showed:
- A **working actor** gets roughly 100–150 agent submissions a year, a handful of call-ins,
  several TV/commercial bookings and rarely a film role. Two of the three careers had no
  film offer in two to three years. Comparable NPCs convert about 15% of auditions.
- An **established actor** gets 50+ auditions a year (mostly episodic TV) and earned
  a film co-lead after about 13 months.
- Hidden traits matter and are discoverable through feedback ("they liked the read but
  wanted someone with more screen presence"), so a player can respond: take classes,
  follow up with casting directors who liked them.
