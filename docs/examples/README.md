# Example narrator packets (real engine output)

**New:** [`initiatives/`](initiatives/README.md) shows the three-level narrator interface on a saved career
(Daniel Brooks, 1980–82): narrator-originated initiatives adjudicated (rejected with the actual date conflict,
a meeting arranged, an original film developed, packaged with a director, put in turnaround, rewritten and
greenlit), his agent's own pushes, and a later packet that keeps all of it in continuity.

Generated by `hsim` on the historical 1980 world, playing turns of four weeks, with
in-character choices made through ordinary reply blocks. Nothing was scripted or
forced. These are the actual packets ChatGPT would receive.

**Provenance labels.** Every real person, film, role and event says what is documented
and what belongs to this simulated timeline:
- films: `historical_record` (real film: what is documented) or `provenance: simulated production`
- co-stars: "character name documented / simulated", "documented casting"
- events: `provenance` such as "documented history", "documented casting (as in history)",
  "simulated (not historical)", "simulated divergence from history"
- people: `recent_work` entries tagged documented / this timeline; relationship status tagged
  documented or simulated; real-person cards say which traits are characterisation

**Regenerated after the narrative-integrity pass.** The social, on-set and partnership rules changed, so
these are new runs of the same careers with the same seeds and choices. The main arc (Claire's audition
year and her *Second Harbor* offer) recurs, while incidental events differ. What's new:
- each career's narrator notes belong to that career only (the earlier Claire packets wrongly carried Nora's note)
- real people's bios contain only facts dated before the start (no death years or later careers)
- the offer and production packets include the film's `story`: conflict, characters, arcs, relationships, beats,
  key scenes, creative intent and Claire's own read of the script
- documented partnerships are protected (Sondra Locke is "in a relationship with Clint Eastwood (documented)")
- premiere invitations state why you were invited; on-set friction needs a concrete cause

## How often do real opportunities come?
Three test careers (about six simulated years in all) showed:
- A **working actor** gets roughly 100–150 agent submissions a year, a handful of call-ins,
  several TV/commercial bookings and rarely a film role. Two of the three careers had no
  film offer in two to three years. Comparable NPCs convert about 15% of auditions.
- An **established actor** gets 50+ auditions a year (mostly episodic TV) and earned
  a film co-lead after about 13 months.
- Hidden traits matter and are discoverable through feedback ("they liked the read but
  wanted someone with more screen presence"), so a player can respond: take classes,
  follow up with casting directors who liked them.
